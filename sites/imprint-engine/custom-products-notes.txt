BEHAVIOR
script.js customProducts() initializes inside [custom-products].
[custom-category-trigger] identifies each category control.
[custom-category-panel] identifies its page-level wrapper and matching value.
Native .custom-category-panel.active shows the selected component only.
These page wrappers preserve the reusable components and their prop identities.
[custom-decoration-filter] selects All or a product type.
[custom-decoration-card] contains the matching space-separated categories.
Switching is immediate. Keyboard arrows, Home, End, Space and Enter work on
category controls. Hidden panels are inert. Height changes refresh Lenis and
ScrollTrigger so the footer stays reachable.

SOURCE ARTWORK
Many image frames in the current Figma category/options/decoration designs are
empty. Their image props remain available. Blank image props are hidden by a
small scoped CSS rule to avoid broken-image icons while preserving the frames.
Material colour swatches are native Webflow styles. Adding an image to its prop
shows it automatically; there is no redundant Show Image toggle.
